Competition & Rivalry

A step-by-step guide to your yearly project. Follow the timeline, complete the tasks, and create something amazing.

1

First Check: Topic Selection

Your Task

  • Carefully read the project description and rubric.

  • Brainstorm 2-3 potential rivalry topics that interest you.

  • Prepare a list of questions about the project.

Expectations

By the end of our meeting, you will have a final, approved rivalry topic and a clear understanding of the project's goals.

2

Second Check: Research & Draft Plan

Your Task

  • Begin research using reliable sources.

  • Create a draft plan or outline for your case study.

  • Compile a list of your sources found so far.

Expectations

We will review your plan and research to ensure you are on the right track and provide feedback to help focus your efforts.

Case Study Focus Areas

Background: Who were the competitors and what was the context of their competition?

Key Events: Describe the major events, achievements, or conflicts that defined their rivalry.

Impact & Outcomes: Analyze the results. What new inventions, records, or ideas emerged?

Quotes: Find statements and present them using both direct and reported speech.

3

Third Check: Rough Draft

Your Task

  • Write the full rough draft of your case study.

  • Integrate quotes using direct and reported speech.

Expectations

I will provide detailed feedback on your writing, focusing on clarity, analysis, and your use of evidence.

Draft Focus Areas

Complete Narrative: Your draft should tell the full story, incorporating all your research on the rivalry's background, key events, and impact.

Evidence & Quotes: Ensure you have integrated your quotes correctly, using both direct and reported speech formats as required.

4

Fourth Check: Final Report & Presentation Plan

Your Task

  • Revise and edit your rough draft into a final version.

  • Create a detailed plan or storyboard for your presentation.

Expectations

Your final written report is due. We will also finalize your presentation plan and set a date.

Presentation Plan Focus Areas

Visual Support: Decide on your format (Sketch, PowerPoint, iMovie) and plan the key visuals, music, or effects you will use.

Structure & Timing: Outline the flow of your presentation, from introduction to conclusion, to fit the 10-15 minute timeframe.

5

Fifth Check: Final Presentation

Your Task

  • Practice your presentation multiple times (10-15 mins).

  • Finalize your visual aids (PowerPoint, iMovie, etc.).

  • Deliver your awesome presentation to the class!

Expectations

A clear, confident, and engaging presentation that demonstrates your deep understanding of the topic, supported by your visual aid.

2 Points

1P: Included but not clearly stated.

0P: Not included.

12 Points

A well-structured report with clear sections is essential.

Content / Development (Explanation & Visuals)

22 Points

Content should be detailed, well-explained, and supported with visuals.

Language and Expression

8 Points

Advanced vocabulary and correct grammar are expected.

Use of Sources (Count, Reliability, Format, Citations)

8 Points

Proper use and citation of multiple reliable sources (APA/MLA).

Planning, Order, and Conclusion

4 Points

Visual Impact & Use of Technology/Sources

Relevance to Content

Project Rules & Grading

General Rules

  • No plagiarism! You must reference all sources.
  • Stick to the check days and the deadline (5 check-ins required).
  • Be ready to present your work orally in class.
  • All work must be your own product without outside help.
  • Papers must be presentable, tidy, and well-written (Calibri, 11pt if typed). Submit in a file with your name, number, and class.
  • Presentations may be supported by a sketch, PowerPoint, or iMovie.

Grading Breakdown

  • Report: 50%
  • Product: 25%
  • Presentation: 25%
